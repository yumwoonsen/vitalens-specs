## Ward geometry and patients

The ward and patient-body models in `Assets/Ward/` and `demo/ward/` were built by the team's own build scripts, which are not published.

The patients in `Assets/Ward/VitalLensWard.glb` (detailed at Bed 2, simplified at Beds 1, 3 and 4) are built in Blender with MPFB from MakeHuman system assets (base mesh, skin `middleage_asian_male`, eyes `brown`, eyebrows `eyebrow010`, eyelashes `eyelashes02`, hair `short02`, clothes `male_casualsuit06`). The MakeHuman project released these assets under CC0 1.0 in September 2020 (http://www.makehumancommunity.org). The patients are fictional.
